02 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Decisions that define the behavior.

01

Bounded remediation

The tutor uses a fixed three-turn sequence — Probe, Guide, Consolidate — so remediation remains purposeful and finite.

02

Independent transfer

Dialogue completion does not equal mastery. A structurally distinct held-out item tests whether the learner can transfer the principle.

03

Shared evidence engine

BM25 and dense retrieval feed RRF, cross-encoder reranking, provenance checks and proposition-level NLI verification.

  • PMCID / DOI provenance
  • Rights gating
  • Claim-level verification
  • SAFE_FALLBACK

04 / SAFETY & RELIABILITY

Generative help is separated from deterministic truth.

The tutor can explain and remediate, but unsupported medical claims fail closed. Exam scoring and anatomy structure scoring remain deterministic.

01

SAFE_FALLBACK when evidence is insufficient.

02

Provenance and rights gating before grounded generation.

03

Proposition-level NLI verification.

04

Canonical anatomy geometry is not generated or altered by AI.

07 / TRADE-OFFS

What is deliberately bounded.

01

The quality signals describe software verification, not educational or clinical efficacy.

02

Bounded dialogue limits flexibility in exchange for predictable remediation behavior.

03

Cross-encoder reranking and NLI verification add latency and operational complexity.

04

The platform is educational and must not be represented as diagnosis or clinical decision support.
